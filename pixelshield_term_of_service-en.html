2. Service Content and Authorization

1) Service Content

This software is a fully offline, local-runtool for protecting original images and verifying file integrity.

Its main features include:

  • Invisible watermark embedding based on image pixel features and digital signal processing technology;
  • Digital Binding (SHA-256) file hash calculation;
  • Local verification record management;
  • Invisible watermark detection and verification;
  • Exporting local Verification Reports.

Specifically：

  • Invisible watermarks help verify the source and integrity of images;
  • SHA-256 hashes are used to check if a file’s content has changed, and don’t legally determine the creator or copyright ownership of the work.

All image processing, verification record generation, and verification are performed locally on your device—no cloud storage or online services are provided.
